  2. KYC — Identity and legal checks for founders and core team.
  3. Review Board — Tokenomics and technical architecture assessment.
  4. Governance Vote — Listing approval by Neighborhood governance.
  5. TGE Launch — Sale deployment, vesting setup, and liquidity handling.
